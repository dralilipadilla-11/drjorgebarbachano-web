Why nose and sleep belong together

A nose operated in isolation can leave the real problem untouched.

The nose sets the tone for the night.

When the nose is blocked, breathing shifts to the mouth. Mouth breathing during sleep is linked to snoring, fragmented sleep and waking up tired.

Surgery alone is rarely the full answer to sleep apnea.

A clearer nose can make snoring and CPAP easier to live with, but sleep apnea has more than one cause. So sleep is measured before a surgical decision is made — not after.

Aesthetics follow function.

Functional rhinoseptoplasty corrects the septum, the nasal valve and the turbinates while harmonizing the external shape. Form follows function — always.

Sometimes the answer is not surgery.

If your evaluation shows that surgery won't change what you feel, we tell you — and we show you what will.

FAQ

What patients ask before traveling to Cancún

How much does a rhinoplasty cost in Cancún, Mexico?

The cost depends on your nasal structure and the scope of the surgery, so it is quoted after your evaluation, not before. The video evaluation costs $100 USD and ends with a written plan: what is causing your symptoms, what is recommended, and what it costs.

Can nose surgery fix my snoring or sleep apnea?

Sometimes it is part of the answer, rarely the whole answer. A blocked nose can make snoring worse and CPAP harder to tolerate, but nasal surgery on its own does not usually resolve moderate or severe obstructive sleep apnea. That is why, in the Riviera Sleep Program, sleep is evaluated before any surgical decision is made.

Do I need a sleep study?

Only if your evaluation points to it. If it does, Dr. Lili Padilla runs it during your stay: a sleep test you take to your hotel room, or a full overnight polysomnography at the Four Points by Sheraton, connected to Hospital Galenia. If you already have a recent sleep study, send it before your video evaluation.

Is the surgery done under general anesthesia? Will I have nasal packing?

No general anesthesia and no nasal packing. Surgery is outpatient, under light sedation, so you go back to your hotel the same day. There is nothing to pull out of your nose afterwards: about a week later the external splint and stitches come off.

How long should I stay in Cancún after nose surgery?

Most patients traveling in plan for 8 to 10 days: outpatient surgery under light sedation, removal of the external splint and stitches around day 7, and a final in-person check before flying home. You stay at the Four Points by Sheraton, connected to Hospital Galenia by a walkway, about 15 minutes from Cancún International Airport.

Do I need to travel for the first consultation?

No. The first evaluation is a video consultation. We review your history, your breathing, your sleep and your goals, and build your plan before you book a flight. No referral needed.

What should I send before my video evaluation?

Anything you already have: a CT scan of the sinuses, a previous sleep study, prior surgery reports and front and profile photos of your nose. None of it is required to book.

Is the consultation available in English?

Yes. Consultations, surgical planning and follow-up are available in English and Spanish. WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach the practice.
